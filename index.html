Crafted with heritage

Saffran Patisserie elevates the neighbourhood dessert experience with a curated collection of Persian-inspired pastries. From the moment you walk through the door, warm hospitality and the aroma of freshly baked delights create a welcoming escape from the everyday.

Our team combines traditional recipes with modern techniques to bring you a seasonal rotation of delicacies. Whether you're sharing Mix Creamy Pastries with friends or gifting Mix Persian Cookies to someone special, each selection is made fresh in-house for an unforgettable tasting journey.
